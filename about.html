About Beyond Borders

"Collect experiences, not expectations."

Our Mission

Beyond Borders (BBSOC) is a King's College London society dedicated to helping students step outside their comfort zone: professionally, personally, and creatively. We bring together career development, wellbeing, culture, and community into one place, so members can grow in every direction, not just academically.

What We Do

From careers fairs and networking nights to wellbeing walks, volunteering days, and skills workshops, BBSOC runs events across five pillars: Career Explorer, Personal Growth, Skill Builder, Community, and Comfort Zone. Members track their journey through the Beyond Borders Passport and unlock rewards as they go.
